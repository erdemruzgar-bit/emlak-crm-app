The Architect's Vision

Nestled along the jagged ridgeline of obsidian cliffs, Midnight Ridge Villa is a masterclass in modern brutalist architecture softened by organic textures. Designed by the award-winning firm Studio Nocturne, the property features cantilevered glass wings that offer 270-degree views of the canyon below.

Every element has been curated for the discerning collector: hand-honed basalt floors, reclaimed cedar ceilings, and a smart-climate system that mimics the natural flow of mountain air. The transition between indoor and outdoor spaces is seamless, centering around a monolithic infinity pool that appears to spill directly into the valley.
